// OPEN-SOURCE CLEAN AIR BLUEPRINT

THE CORSI-ROSENTHAL BOX BLUEPRINT (CADR 400+ CFM)

Developed by environmental engineer Dr. Richard Corsi and Jim Rosenthal. A DIY Corsi-Rosenthal cube delivers higher clean air turnover than purifiers costing 5x as much, while reducing filter air resistance and motor strain.

STEP 01
BILL OF MATERIALS
• 1x 20-inch Box Fan (3-speed, standard motor).
• 4x MERV 13 Furnace Filters (20" x 20" x 1" or 2").
• 1x Roll Heavy-Duty Duct Tape.
• 1x 20" x 20" Cardboard Base Shroud.
STEP 02
ASSEMBLY CUBE
Tape the 4 filters into a cube with arrows pointing inward toward the center. Tape the cardboard square as the bottom floor. Mount the fan on top blowing UPWARD to pull air through all 4 pleated sides simultaneously.
STEP 03
CARDBOARD SHROUD MAGIC
Cut a 14-inch circular donut hole in cardboard and tape it over the fan face. This prevents blade corner vortex backflow, boosting Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) by 40% with zero extra electricity.
